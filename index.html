DDD Intelligence

A conversational, local-first assistant for Digital Dollar Dominance. Chat naturally; each turn still uses the frozen snapshot, retrieved notes, and QVAC on your machine.

Research tool, not advice DDD Intelligence is a research and demonstration tool only. It is not investment, financial, legal, tax, or trading advice, and it is not a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any asset, issuer, chain, or token.
  • Answers are generated by a small local language model and may be incomplete, outdated, or factually wrong.
  • The local snapshot is a frozen copy of public DDD data and may not reflect current market conditions.
  • Stablecoins, on-chain assets, and prediction markets carry material risk, including total loss of principal, smart-contract failure, depeg, regulatory action, and counterparty default.
  • Verify every figure against the original sources (DeFiLlama, FRED M2SL, issuer disclosures) before relying on it.
  • Use of this tool is at your own risk. No warranty, express or implied, is provided.
GitHub snapshot cadence The JSON snapshot files shipped with this repository (for example data/ddd-current.snapshot.json and data/ddd-onchain.snapshot.json) are published on a six-hour cadence when automation pushes updates to GitHub. What you see in a local checkout or build is whatever snapshot was last committed—always compare last_updated in the JSON to your needs. This UI does not silently refresh from the network while you browse.
