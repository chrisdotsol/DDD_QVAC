# Global Stablecoin & Crypto Regulation Overview 2026

- **United States**: GENIUS Act / stablecoin bills advancing. 1099-DA reporting. SEC vs. CFTC jurisdiction ongoing.
- **European Union**: MiCA fully in force — strict licensing for stablecoin issuers, reserve requirements.
- **United Kingdom**: FCA stablecoin regime expanding. CARF reporting from 2026.
- **Asia**: Singapore (MAS sandbox), Hong Kong (licensed issuers), Japan (strict but clear).
- **UAE**: VARA in Dubai — progressive licensing, tax-friendly.
- **Latin America**: El Salvador pro-crypto; Brazil regulation maturing.

**Trend**: Increasing focus on reserve transparency, consumer protection, and AML. Compliant issuers (Circle, Paxos) gaining institutional share.

**For Users**: KYC/AML on centralized platforms is standard. Self-custody reduces some reporting but not tax obligations.
