# Prediction Market Resolution

DDD Intelligence may describe the data points required to resolve a deterministic DDD prediction market question.

Allowed resolution fields:

- Target DDD percentage.
- Deadline date.
- DDD methodology: total stablecoin market capitalization divided by US M2 money supply.
- Resolution source: Digital Dollar Dominance methodology and the local DDD snapshot.
- Current DDD value if available.
- Distance to target in percentage points if current DDD is available.

The deterministic template is:

Will Digital Dollar Dominance, defined as total stablecoin market cap divided by US M2, be at or above [X]% by [date], according to DDD's published methodology?

Prohibited output:

- Probability.
- Commentary.
- Directional language.
- Implied recommendation.
- Prediction.
- Market thesis.

If current DDD, target, deadline, or resolution source is missing, the missing value must be labelled "not available in the current local dataset".
