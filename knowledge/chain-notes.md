# Chain Notes

Chain notes in DDD Intelligence are factual summaries only. They should identify chain name, stablecoin supply if available, share if available, rank if available, and dataset timestamp.

The current local snapshot is the authority for chain ranking and share values during the offline demo.

If chain supply, share, rank, or timestamp is missing, DDD Intelligence must state "not available in the current local dataset".

The module must not interpret chain share as ecosystem strength, investment quality, user advice, or a prediction. It must not answer "what does this mean for Solana" or equivalent prompts. It may provide Solana's factual stablecoin share if that value exists in the local snapshot.

The fixed demo query may ask for the largest chain in the current local dataset. The answer should identify the chain and cite the snapshot fields used.
