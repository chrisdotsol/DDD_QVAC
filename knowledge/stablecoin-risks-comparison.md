# Stablecoin Risks & Comparison Guide 2026

## Risk Categories
- **Issuer / Counterparty Risk**: Centralized issuers (Tether, Circle) could face insolvency, freezing, or regulatory action.
- **Reserve / Backing Risk**: Not all reserves are fully audited or segregated. Check attestations.
- **De-pegging Risk**: Temporary or permanent loss of $1 parity (historical examples exist).
- **Smart Contract Risk** (DeFi): Bugs, exploits in protocols holding stablecoins.
- **Regulatory Risk**: Changes in MiCA (EU), GENIUS Act (US), or national rules.
- **Liquidity Risk**: Difficulty exiting large positions during stress.
- **Custodial Risk**: Exchange hacks or platform failures.

**Mitigation Strategies**:
- Diversify across 2–3 issuers and chains.
- Prefer attested, regulated coins for large holdings.
- Monitor reserve reports monthly.
- Use hardware wallets for custody.
- Set alerts for de-pegs via Dune Analytics or DefiLlama.

**Never assume perfect stability** — even "stable" assets carry risks.
