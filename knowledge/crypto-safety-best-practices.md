# General Crypto Safety Best Practices 2026

- **Seed Phrase**: The single point of failure. Treat like a physical key to a vault. 3+ offline copies in secure locations.
- **2FA**: Hardware keys (Yubikey) preferred over SMS.
- **Phishing**: Verify URLs, domain names, and contract addresses.
- **Social Engineering**: No one from "support" will ever ask for your seed.
- **Device Security**: Dedicated clean device for crypto if possible.
- **Portfolio Size**: Only invest what you can afford to lose. Dollar-cost average.
- **Backup & Recovery**: Test restores periodically with small amounts.
- **Monitoring**: Use tools like Zerion, DeBank, or blockchain alerts.
- **Legal**: Comply with KYC/AML where required. Understand local regulations.

**Golden Rule**: Not your keys, not your coins. Self-custody responsibly.
