# Stablecoin Glossary

Stablecoin: A crypto asset designed to maintain a reference value, commonly one US dollar.

Stablecoin market capitalization: The aggregate dollar value of stablecoins in circulation. In DDD, this is the numerator of the DDD ratio.

US M2 money supply: A broad measure of US dollar money supply that includes currency, checking deposits, savings deposits, and other liquid money-like balances. In DDD, this is the denominator of the DDD ratio.

One in X figure: A plain-language expression of the DDD ratio. If DDD is about 1.4 percent, the corresponding statement is approximately one in 71 US dollars is represented by stablecoins.

Issuer share: The share of tracked stablecoin supply represented by a specific issuer or asset in the local dataset.

Chain share: The share of tracked stablecoin supply represented on a specific blockchain in the local dataset.

Frozen demo snapshot: A local JSON dataset generated before the offline demo. It is used so the demo does not depend on live network calls.
