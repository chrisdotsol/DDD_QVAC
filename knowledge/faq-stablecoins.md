# Stablecoin FAQ 2026

**Q: Are stablecoins safe?**  
A: Safer than volatile crypto for value storage, but not risk-free. Issuer and regulatory risks remain.

**Q: Can I earn yield on stablecoins?**  
A: Yes — via lending protocols, but with risks.

**Q: Which chain is best?**  
A: Ethereum (most secure/liquid), Solana (fast/cheap), Base (low fees). Diversify.

**Q: How do I redeem for fiat?**  
A: Through issuer portals (often with KYC) or exchanges.

**Q: Tax on stablecoin interest?**  
A: Usually ordinary income in most jurisdictions.

**Q: What if a stablecoin de-pegs?**  
A: Sell quickly or wait for recovery. Diversification helps.

**Always DYOR and consult professionals.**
