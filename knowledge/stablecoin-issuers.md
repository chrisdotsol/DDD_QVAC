# Major Stablecoin Issuers & Assets (2026 Overview)

**Data as of early-mid 2026**. Market caps fluctuate. Always check latest attestations.

## Top Issuers & Stablecoins
1. **Tether (USDT)** – Issuer: Tether Limited
   - Largest by supply (~$180B+).
   - Backing: Cash, U.S. Treasuries, reverse repos, other assets.
   - Attestations: Quarterly (BDO). Real-time dashboard available.
   - Widely available on Tron, Ethereum, Solana, etc.

2. **Circle (USDC)**
   - Supply: ~$70B+.
   - Backing: Cash & short-duration U.S. Treasuries (1:1, segregated).
   - Monthly attestations (Grant Thornton). Strong regulatory compliance (U.S. licenses).
   - Preferred for institutions.

3. **MakerDAO (DAI)**
   - Decentralized, crypto-collateralized (~$5B+).
   - Over-collateralized vaults (ETH, BTC, RWAs).

4. **PayPal USD (PYUSD)** – Issuer: Paxos
   - Backed by USD deposits & Treasuries.
   - Monthly attestations. Integrated with PayPal ecosystem.

5. **First Digital (FDUSD)**
   - Popular on Binance/BNB Chain.
   - Cash & equivalents, attested.

6. **Others**: Ethena USDe (synthetic), USDS, RLUSD, etc.

**Key Risks by Type**:
- **Fiat-backed**: Issuer/ reserve / redemption risk.
- **Crypto-backed**: Liquidation & volatility risk.
- **Algorithmic**: De-pegging history (e.g., older UST).

**Transparency Tip**: Review monthly/quarterly attestations on issuer websites. Prefer regulated, attested issuers for large holdings.
