# How to Use Stablecoins Safely (2026 Best Practices)

1. **Choose Reputable Stablecoins**: Prioritize USDC, PYUSD, or attested fiat-backed over unverified alternatives for core holdings.
2. **Diversify**: Don't put everything in one issuer or chain.
3. **Self-Custody**: Move off exchanges to non-custodial wallets for large amounts.
4. **Verify Transactions**:
   - Double-check addresses, contract addresses, and amounts.
   - Use blockchain explorers (Etherscan, Solscan).
5. **Yield Farming / Lending Risks**: Smart contract, platform, and de-pegging risks. Start small; use audited protocols.
6. **Redemption**: Understand issuer redemption processes (KYC may apply for large amounts).
7. **Taxes & Compliance**: Track every movement.
8. **Scam Awareness**: Never click unsolicited links, approve unknown contracts, or share seed phrases.

**General Rule**: If it sounds too good to be true (guaranteed high yields), it probably is.
